JobMatch AI
Building AI course project
Summary
JobMatch AI is an AI-based tool that helps students find part-time jobs that fit their schedule, location, skills and interests. The AI compares the student's information with job advertisements and recommends the most suitable jobs.
Background
Finding a part-time job can be difficult for students. There are many job advertisements, but not all of them fit around school.
For example, a student might only be able to work after 15:00 on weekdays. Some jobs may require morning shifts, so they would not be suitable even if the student has the right skills.
JobMatch AI would make the job search easier by finding jobs that match the student's situation.
The idea is important because students often need work experience and income, but finding a suitable job can take a lot of time.
How is it used?
First, the student gives the AI some information about themselves.
For example:
when they are available to work
where they want to work
how many hours they want to work
what type of job they are looking for
what skills and previous work experience they have
whether they can travel by car or public transport
The AI then compares this information with job advertisements.
The system could give each job a suitability score. For example:
95% – very suitable
80% – suitable
60% – possibly suitable
30% – not very suitable
The student can then look at the recommended jobs and decide which ones they want to apply for.
Data sources and AI methods
The system would need information from job advertisements and information provided by the user.
Job advertisements could include:
location
working hours
number of working hours
job type
required skills
required experience
salary, if available
The AI could use classification and recommendation methods to compare the user's information with the requirements of different jobs.
For example, if a student is only available after 15:00, the AI would recommend jobs that start after that time instead of jobs that require morning shifts.
The AI would only make recommendations. The final decision would always be made by the user.
Challenges
One challenge is that job advertisements do not always contain all the information about a job.
The AI could also make mistakes if the information provided by the user or the employer is incomplete or unclear.
Privacy would also be important. Personal information should be handled safely and only used for the purpose the user has agreed to.
Another challenge is fairness. The AI should not discriminate against people based on personal characteristics that are not relevant to the job.
What next?
In the future, JobMatch AI could have more features.
For example, it could:
help users create a CV
help write job applications
remind users about application deadlines
keep track of jobs they have applied for
recommend new jobs based on previous searches
compare the user's skills with the requirements of a job
The project could also become a real application or website that students can use when looking for work.
